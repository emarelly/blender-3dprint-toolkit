G9 dome adapter: section sketch

One printed PETG part that holds the glass. A Ø60 dome sits on top of the globe over the Ø40 cut top, with a Ø13 bore in its hub. Below it, a spigot screws into the glass's internal thread and carries the M10×1 pipe thread inside, so the pipe's short threaded end reaches it. The pipe end continues into the globe, and the G9 holder screws onto it to hold the bulb. Values in red are still unmeasured, so they are drawn as placeholders.

Assembly order

  1. Fit the O-ring into the groove under the dome.
  2. Screw the adapter's spigot into the glass until the dome presses the O-ring onto the glass.
  3. Feed the pipe (wires inside) down through the hub: it slides through the Ø13 bore and its threaded end screws into the M10×1 thread in the spigot, then out into the globe.
  4. Connect the G9 holder and screw it onto the pipe end inside the globe. The pipe thread must be long enough for the 15 mm of adapter thread plus the holder.

Printing

Flat top of the dome on the bed, spigot pointing up. No supports needed. Both threads print upright.
