G9 dome adapter: section sketch

One printed PETG part that holds the glass. The spigot screws into the glass's internal G9 thread and has the M10×1 pipe thread inside. The Ø60 dome on top covers the globe's Ø36 flat cut top. A 32×2 O-ring in its flat underside seals on the glass. The pipe comes in from above, through the Ø13 bore and the M10×1 thread, and the G9 holder screws onto its end inside the globe.

Assembly order

  1. Press the 32×2 O-ring into the groove in the flat underside.
  2. Screw the spigot into the glass by hand until the flat underside rests on the glass (you feel it stop). The O-ring is then squeezed 25 %.
  3. Feed the pipe (wires inside) down through the Ø13 bore and screw it through the M10×1 thread. About 5 mm of its 20 mm thread comes out below.
  4. Screw the G9 holder onto the pipe end inside the globe. It holds the bulb only.
  5. On top: a rubber washer in the Ø15 recess, then an M10×1 nut.

Printing

Flat dome top on the bed, spigot pointing up, no supports, 0.12 mm layers. Both threads print upright. The first ~2 mm of the R8 edge overhang at the bed: add a brim if it curls. Print the spigot alone first to check the glass thread.
