Vacuum bag adapter: sketch

One printed PETG part. Its 39.5 × 17.3 oval spigot (copied from another vacuum adapter that fits) pushes 30 mm into the portable vacuum. A 20 mm transition turns the oval into a round neck. The round socket on top has the bag valve's real thread and screws onto the valve's male inlet (Ø23.5–25.1). That thread is two-start: two threads 180° apart, 1.0 mm between ridges, 2.0 mm lead, Ø24.4 / 26.6. The valve's rim seals flat against the Ø21 shoulder. Drawn in print orientation: oval end on the bed (Z = 0), thread end up.

v1.3: the two-start adapter (vacuum_bag_adapter_2start_build.py), tested and working, is drawn here. The first adapter (vacuum_bag_adapter_build.py) has a single-start P1.5 thread; the coupler below lets it fit the valve.

Dimensions

Use

  1. Screw the adapter onto the bag valve by hand until the valve rim stops on the shoulder (up to about 3½ turns).
  2. Push the oval spigot into the vacuum, up to 30 mm deep.
  3. Run the vacuum; the bag valve closes when you unscrew the adapter.

Printing

PETG, 0.4 nozzle, 0.16 mm layers. Oval end on the bed, thread end up, no supports. The transition leans at most about 18° (limit 45°). The 1.0 mm lead-in chamfer at the oval tip doubles as the elephant's-foot chamfer. The seal shoulder faces up and prints flat. The two-start thread is the finest feature: its ridges are about 0.5 mm wide at half height, close to one line, so the report's min_wall flag on them is expected. Tune the fits with SPIGOT_ADJ / THREAD_ADJ.

For the first adapter (single-start P1.5 socket). Screw the coupler's male end into that adapter until its end face stops on the adapter's Ø21 shoulder (about 4½ turns). Then screw the pair onto the bag valve until the valve rim stops on the coupler's shoulder (about 3½ turns). It prints male end down, with no supports: the outer chamfer is exactly 45°.
